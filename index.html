    Social campaign · Tropical Smoothie Cafe

    The Free Smoothie Raffle

    A promotion I designed and ran as General Manager of Tropical Smoothie Cafe at Geneva Commons. It ran for 3 months with a drawing every week. To enter, a customer posted a photo of their smoothie to their own Instagram story, tagged the store and used #FreeSmoothieRaffle; each week's winner got a free smoothie every day for a week. It worked because the cafe spread through customers' own followers — people pay far more attention to a friend's post than to an ad a stranger scrolls past.

    70–90entries a week
    $14a week in cost
    10.5–13.5kpotential views a week
    ~$1per 1,000 views — vs ≈$13.66 for paid Instagram ads

    Buying that same reach as paid Instagram ads would have cost about $143–$184 a week — roughly $1,865–$2,397 over the whole campaign. The raffle's total cost was $182. (View counts are estimates, based on entrants' follower counts.)

    Brand identity · Financial startup

    FinC-Lamp

    A complete logo and brand guide for a financial services startup concept. It started as my pen sketch of a laughing, mustached character in a fez with crossed arms. I reduced the character to one continuous line — the crossed arms and posture stay, but the body becomes a genie rising from a lamp — then paired it with a hand-lettered script wordmark. The finished work was delivered as vector files with a brand guide covering the logo versions, clear space, minimum sizes, color, type and what not to do with it, so printers, sign shops and future designers can use the brand correctly without guesswork.

    Lamp Teal #358E9C Night Navy #2B3582 Genie Red #D0304E Smoke Purple #563488

    The brand guide, broken down

    Logo versions
    Primary in full color, the icon on its own, reversed white on Night Navy, and one color for stamps and engraving.
    Clear space & minimum size
    Keep at least half the icon's width empty on every side. Minimum size: full logo 1 in wide in print (96 px on screen); icon 0.4 in (32 px). Below that, use the icon alone.
    Gradients
    The ring runs Lamp Teal → Night Navy, left to right. The genie and the wordmark run Genie Red → Smoke Purple.
    Typography
    Familjen Grotesk is the supporting typeface for headings, menus and documents. The script wordmark is hand-lettered artwork — always use the logo file, never retype it.
    Misuse rules
    Don't stretch or squash it, don't recolor it, don't rotate it, and don't place it on busy backgrounds.

    Web app · Product & development

    Fantasy Flops

    A fantasy football draft assistant that works on your phone and installs to the home screen. During a live draft it tells you who to pick next and why — showing its reasoning instead of hiding it behind a single ranking number. After the draft it becomes a roster and trade analysis tool. I designed the product and the logic behind every recommendation, then built and tested it with AI-assisted development tools.

    • Turn-aware picks. Estimates which players will be gone before your next turn, and what waiting would cost you.
    • Coach and What-if. Calls out weak spots in your roster and shows what each pick gives up at other positions.
    • Live sync. Follows Sleeper drafts automatically, with a manual mode and screenshot import that reads player names on the device.
    • Private by design. Works offline — no accounts, no tracking, no data leaving the phone.
